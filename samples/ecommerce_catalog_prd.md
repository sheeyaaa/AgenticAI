# E-commerce Catalog PRD
Visitors can search products by keyword, filter by category and non-negative price range, and sort results by relevance or price.
Visitors can add available products to the cart, update quantities within stock limits, and see updated subtotals and totals.
Invalid or expired discount codes show an error without changing the cart total.
Customers must enter a valid shipping address before checkout, and successful orders display a confirmation number.
